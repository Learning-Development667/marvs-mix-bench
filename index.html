How the Bench decides
  • A scale can be out by half its smallest step. To keep that error under about 5%, each tinter must weigh at least ten steps: 10 g on a 1 g scale, 1 g on a 0.1 g scale.
  • Anything lighter is converted to millilitres using the density printed on the tin, for a syringe. Below 0.10 ml a 1 ml syringe is not accurate enough either.
  • Tinters are thick and some stays in the syringe. Use the same technique every time.
  • Syringe sizes and the 0.10 ml limit are working assumptions until the stockroom confirms what technicians carry.
